2Scope of work

I will design, build and launch the project described above. What each package includes:

  • Basic: up to 5 pages, mobile-friendly design, contact form, map, WhatsApp button and basic Google SEO.
  • Standard: everything in Basic, plus an admin panel so you can edit content yourself, search and filters, and hosting and database setup.
  • Premium: everything in Standard, plus customer accounts, online payments, and an Android app published on Google Play.

Anything not listed in the project details (for example extra pages, new features, logo design or writing your text) is extra work. I will always give you the price first, and only start once you agree.

4Timeline

  • The timeline starts when I have received both the first payment and your content (see section 5).
  • Typical delivery: Basic about 1 week, Standard 2–3 weeks, Premium 3–5 weeks.
  • If content or feedback arrives late, the delivery date moves by the same number of days.
  • Google Play reviews new apps on its own schedule. App approval time is outside my control.

5What I need from you

  • Your text, logo, photos, prices and contact details, ready to use.
  • Feedback within 7 days each time I share a design or preview.
  • One person who can make decisions for your business.
  • Your own domain and hosting accounts, in your name. I can help you set them up.
  • For Premium: your own Google Play developer account and payment gateway account (PayPal, Stripe or similar), in your name.

You confirm that you have the right to use all content you send me (text, images, logos).

6Revisions

  • Basic includes 2 rounds of changes. Standard and Premium include 3 rounds.
  • One round means one list of changes sent together, not one change at a time.
  • Changes after the included rounds, or changes to something already approved, are quoted as extra work.

7Preview and launch

  • While I build, you can follow progress on a private preview link.
  • When you approve the final preview and the final payment is received, I launch the project on your domain and hand over all logins, admin access and files.
  • Until the final payment, the project stays on my preview link.

8Ownership

  • After full payment, you own the website, its design and your content.
  • Open-source tools, fonts and libraries used in the project stay under their own free licences.
  • I may show the finished project in my portfolio. If you prefer that I don't, tell me and I won't.

9Support after launch

  • For 30 days after launch, I fix any errors in the work I delivered, free of charge.
  • This covers things not working as agreed. New features or design changes are extra work.
  • After 30 days, ongoing updates and support are available at a price we agree.

10Third-party costs

These are paid by you, directly to the provider, so the accounts always belong to you:

  • Domain name and hosting or server.
  • Google Play developer account (one-time fee charged by Google).
  • Payment gateway fees (PayPal, Stripe and similar take a small fee on each sale).
  • Any paid themes, plugins, images or services you choose to use.

11Cancellation and refunds

  • Before I start work: you can cancel and receive a full refund of the first payment.
  • After work has started: the first payment is not refundable, because it covers work already done.
  • Payments for stages already completed are not refundable.
  • If I am unable to finish the project, I refund payments for any stage I have not delivered.
  • If I hear nothing from you for 30 days, the project is paused. After 60 days without contact, it is closed and payments made so far cover the work done.

12Confidentiality

I keep your business information, passwords and customer data private, and use them only for your project. After handover, I recommend you change any passwords I used.

13Limits of responsibility

  • I am not responsible for problems caused by third-party services, such as hosting outages, payment gateway issues or Google Play decisions.
  • I am not responsible for changes made to the site by you or another person after handover.
  • My total responsibility for any claim is limited to the amount you have paid for the project.
