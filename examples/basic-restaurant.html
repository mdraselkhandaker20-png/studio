Two families, one kitchen

Nasi & Co. began when two neighbours, one from Kelantan and one from Sylhet, started swapping lunch boxes. In 2021 they opened this shop together. The recipes are still the ones their mothers taught them.

  • Halal certified by JAKIM
  • No MSG, spices ground fresh daily
  • Private room for 20 guests
